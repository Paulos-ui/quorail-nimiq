# Submission draft

Category: Pay.

Tagline: See what a payment leaves behind.

Description:
Quorail helps independent workers and small teams plan NIM payments around their existing commitments. Set a reserve, preview a payment's effect on the remaining buffer, then approve it inside Nimiq Pay. Matching on-chain receipts remain attached to their commitments.

Weekly and monthly commitments support 30, 60 and 90 day projections without automatic debits. Optional shared boards track responsibility and verified payments, while split collection requests show partial and complete contributions. Quorail AI uses Anthropic to explain planning questions with optional numeric context and explicit consent. Practice games include chess against a local bot.

Private plans stay on the device and can be exported. Shared snapshots expire after 30 days. Every real payment requires wallet approval. Quorail does not hold funds or promise rewards.
