# One-minute recording guide

Record the real deployed app inside Nimiq Pay. Prepare a small commitment to a second account you control and enough balance above the reserve. Do not expose recovery words or API keys. Network confirmation may take longer than the outline; do not present pending as confirmed.

0-10 seconds: "A wallet shows my balance. Quorail shows how much is already committed." Open Overview and point to the reserve signal and buffer.

10-20 seconds: "I can plan rent and recurring costs before making another payment." Show a monthly commitment and switch the 30/60/90-day projection once.

20-35 seconds: "Here is the payment, what it leaves, and the commitments still ahead." Open the prepared payment. Point to amount, remaining balance and reserve. Tap Pay with Nimiq Pay.

35-45 seconds: Personally inspect and approve the real native wallet transaction. "I approve every payment in my own wallet. Quorail never holds my keys."

45-60 seconds, or longer if the network needs it: Show the returned hash and pending state, then actual verified confirmation. Open Activity and its explorer link. "The receipt stays with the commitment. The next recurring occurrence is created after confirmation."

Optional second clip: Show the landing robot, ask a generic question, then show a shared collection board before and after a real contributor pays. Explain that shared boards are snapshots and incoming contributions do not pay the original outgoing bill.

Use actual screen recordings. Do not manufacture payment receipts, AI answers or network confirmations. If confirmation takes longer, keep the longer recording or label any cut accurately.
